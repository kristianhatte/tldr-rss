When touch makes sense on a laptop (4 minute read)

2026-11-11T00:00:00.000Z

Touchscreen laptops can make certain interactions faster than using a trackpad, particularly when selecting large targets or performing brief, direct actions. In a single-participant experiment across six tasks, touch averaged 1.39 seconds compared with 1.74 seconds for the trackpad, while previous research found similar advantages for some interactions. Touch remains less accurate for small targets and can cause fatigue during extended use, making it better suited as a complementary input method rather than a replacement for traditional laptop controls.
