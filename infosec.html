2026-11-04T00:00:00.000Z

Attackers map who sits in Domain Admins with SharpHound or PowerView long before they change any group membership, and an audit entry (SACL) on the group's member attribute turns those reads into Event 4662, or 4799 for built-in groups, on the domain controller that answered. On real privileged groups, the signal drowns in reads from directory sync, Exchange, and admin tooling, and SAMR-based tools like net group /domain bypass it entirely.
