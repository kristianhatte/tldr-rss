Fit height to background pattern size with calc-size() (4 minute read)

Combining `calc-size()` with `round()` instead lets the browser calculate the element's natural height and round it up to the nearest multiple of the tile height. The result behaves similarly to `min-height`, preserving both the content-driven size and the original pattern proportions, though browser support remains limited.
